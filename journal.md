## 2026-10-21: Front northwest corner, first plantings

Planted 5 in the bed right of the path: 2 tufted hair grass (*Deschampsia*) and 3 sword ferns. Plants came from Go Natives! (10-17 neighborhood sale and 10-18).

**Layout**
- The existing rose isn't centered the way the design assumed, so I'm adjusting a little to fill in around it.
- Tufted hair grass is in the corners for some structure, about 2 ft in from the corner.
- Sword ferns are about 3 ft apart, set in front of the linden and a little closer to the trunk than the plan shows. Sword ferns can look really nice right next to a tree. The 4th sword fern will go behind the linden; not planted yet.

**Planting method**
- The mulch up here is very thick. It's doing its job adding organic matter, but I filled the holes with potting soil so the roots touch soil and not just sticks, with no big air pockets.
- Watered them in well. We'll see how it goes.
