# Lady Fern

## Basic information
- **Scientific name:** Athyrium filix-femina var. cyclosorum
- **Plant type:** Fern
- **USDA zones:** 4-9
- **Native region:** Pacific Northwest; British Columbia south to California, east to Montana

## Growth characteristics
- **Mature height:** 3-5 feet (up to 6 feet in optimal conditions)
- **Mature spread:** 2-3 feet
- **Growth rate:** Medium
- **Lifespan:** Long-lived perennial
- **Roots:** Short, stout rhizome; spreads slowly to form small colonies

## Growing conditions
- **Sun requirements:** Part Shade/Full Shade (tolerates more sun with consistent moisture)
- **Water needs:** Medium-High (needs consistent moisture; not drought tolerant)
- **Soil type:** Moist, well-drained, rich, slightly acidic
- **Soil pH:** 5.0-6.5
- **Native habitat:** Moist woods, forest edges, meadows, shaded riparian corridors; low to middle elevations

## Seasonal interest
- **Bloom time:** N/A (fern)
- **Bloom color:** N/A
- **Fall color:** Deciduous; fronds yellow and die back in fall
- **Winter interest:** None (fully dormant)

## Wildlife value
- **Attracts:** Insects, small mammals
- **Host plant for:** N/A
- **Provides:** Cover and shelter for ground-dwelling birds and small mammals

## Care & maintenance
- **Pruning needs:** Remove dead fronds in late winter before new growth emerges
- **Fertilizer:** Light compost amendment at planting; generally not needed once established
- **Mulch:** Leaf litter or light organic mulch to retain moisture
- **Special care:** Keep soil consistently moist; mulch heavily in first season to establish

## Notes
- **Design notes:** Tall, arching, bright green fronds provide lush texture; effective contrast with sword fern and evergreen groundcovers
- **Observations:**
- **Challenges:** Not drought tolerant; needs reliable moisture
