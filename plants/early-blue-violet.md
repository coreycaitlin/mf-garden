# Early Blue Violet

## Basic information
- **Scientific name:** Viola adunca
- **Plant type:** Herbaceous Perennial
- **USDA zones:** 4-9 (sources vary; ranges from 1-9 to 3b-9b are also listed)
- **Native region:** Most of western North America, Alaska to California; east across Canada and the northern United States

## Growth characteristics
- **Mature height:** Low; about 4-10 inches (sources vary)
- **Mature spread:** Spreads slowly into loose clumps or patches
- **Growth rate:**
- **Lifespan:**
- **Roots:** Slender rhizomes

## Growing conditions
- **Sun requirements:** Full Sun/Part Shade (sun on the coast, light shade inland)
- **Water needs:** Medium; prefers moist soil but tolerates some dryness
- **Soil type:** Well-drained, sandy to loamy
- **Soil pH:**
- **Native habitat:** Meadows, prairies, streambanks and open woods, dry to moist sites, at all elevations

## Seasonal interest
- **Bloom time:** Spring, sometimes persisting into summer
- **Bloom color:** Blue to deep purple
- **Fall color:**
- **Winter interest:**

## Wildlife value
- **Attracts:** Early-season pollinators
- **Host plant for:** Fritillary butterflies, including the endangered Oregon silverspot, whose larvae depend on it
- **Provides:** Nectar
